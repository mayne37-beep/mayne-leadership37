Leading Like Lasso

Thirty moments from Ted Lasso. Examine the leadership choice, understand why it matters, and turn it into a useful action in your organization.

Watch. Understand. Apply.

  1. Notice the behavior, not just the dialogue.
  2. Explain how it could affect people and the work.
  3. Choose an action and check its effect.

For coaches, team leaders, and anyone leading an organization. Browse in any order, or work through all 30.

Start with a leadership challenge

This course is a companion to the 30 leadership teaching moments. It uses actual scenes from seasons 1–3 as discussion cases. The leadership explanations and organizational examples are our interpretations; a fictional scene cannot prove that copying a behavior will produce the same result.

  • Identify observable actions and separate them from your assumptions.
  • Connect a leadership behavior to a possible effect on trust, capability, or performance.
  • Adapt the behavior to your setting and check what actually changes.

The scenes and explanations contain plot spoilers. Some clips may include strong language or distressing family conflict. You can read the explanation without watching.

Choose a scene that connects to this challenge. Return to it as you develop your practice plan.
