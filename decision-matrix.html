Decision Matrix

Compare as many options and criteria as you need. Score each option from 1 to 5, adjust the importance of each criterion, and see how the options rank.

  1. Rename the options.
  2. Enter scores from 1 to 5.
  3. Choose weights and compare results.

Starting weights: Impact × 5, Feasibility × 3, Sustainability × 2. This tool supports your judgment; it doesn't replace it.

Use results to start a discussion
