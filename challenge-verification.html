Measured limits and practical notes

100 simultaneous participants is the verified target; the configured cap is 150 and higher capacity is not claimed. The production run measured question-notification arrival at a 251ms median, 692ms 95th percentile and 3,108ms maximum from the host request. Answer acceptance measured 486ms median, 3,671ms 95th percentile and 6,344ms maximum. The 100 simultaneous joining requests measured 5,278ms median and 7,066ms 95th percentile. Timings include the external testing proxy and service network, and are environment-specific.

The successful load run establishes WebSocket connections in groups of 20, then keeps all 100 active throughout the 25 questions. All 100 students fetch each round and submit concurrently. An initial connection burst exceeded the runner subscription timeout; the successful runner allows 45 seconds for connection setup.

Self-paced identity and attempts are per browser. Clearing cookies or using another browser creates a new identity. Media uploads are public classroom resources; exports retain references. Retention runs when opening the instructor dashboard. Physical device farms, every screen reader, and capacity beyond 100 are outside the verified scope.

Source, additive migrations, environment configuration, machine-readable results and reproducible checks are maintained in the existing website repository. The instructor guide explains ordinary content management without code.
