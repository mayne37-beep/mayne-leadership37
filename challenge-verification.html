Measured limits and practical notes

100 simultaneous participants is the verified target; the configured cap is 150 and higher capacity is not claimed. The repeated-round local run measured a 57ms median notification arrival and 3,125ms 95th percentile from the host request, including initial identity checks. Answer acceptance measured 456ms median and 4,393ms 95th percentile through the development network. These measurements are environment-specific.

Self-paced identity and attempts are per browser. Clearing cookies or using another browser creates a new identity. Media uploads are public classroom resources; exports retain references. Retention runs when opening the instructor dashboard. Physical device farms, every screen reader, and capacity beyond 100 are outside the verified scope.

Source, additive migrations, environment configuration and reproducible checks are maintained in the existing website repository. The instructor guide explains ordinary content management without code.
