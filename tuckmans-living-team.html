THE LEADERSHIP MODEL

What the five stages mean

Originally developed by Bruce Tuckman, with Adjourning subsequently added with Mary Ann Jensen. Stages describe patterns, not a mandatory sequence: real teams may loop back.

01 · Forming

Uncertainty, politeness and a search for purpose, roles and direction.

02 · Storming

Differences become visible. Disagreements around priorities, influence and process emerge.

03 · Norming

The team develops shared expectations, resolves differences and strengthens trust.

04 · Performing

Members coordinate effectively, adapt, take ownership and deliver.

05 · Adjourning

People transition, transfer knowledge, celebrate work and bring relationships to closure.
