MAYNE LEADERSHIP · INSTRUCTOR GUIDE

Turn participation into a teaching moment.

Leadership Challenge Studio stores your games and class results online. Students join on their phones without accounts or email addresses.

Your first live game

  1. Open Classroom Tools → Leadership Challenge Studio. Use your website access PIN, then sign in with your existing Mayne Leadership 360 instructor account.
  2. Open My Games. Choose one of the three editable 10-question examples, or select Create new game.
  3. Enter a title, add questions, choose correct answers, timers and points, then select Publish game. Drafts save automatically; wait for the saved status before closing your browser.
  4. Select Play, choose a mode and settings, and create the joining PIN. Project the lobby or share that browser window in Microsoft Teams.
  5. Ask students to scan the QR code, open the joining link, or visit mayneleadership.com/challenge-join.html and enter the six-digit PIN. Their nicknames appear in your lobby.
  6. Select Start game. Pause when you need to explain. Reveal answers, discuss results, and select the next question. The last question leads to the podium.
  7. Open Game Reports to inspect understanding, review written answers, compare deliveries, and download CSV or Excel. Use Print for a classroom handout.

Build and maintain your own content

Use the question panel to select any question. Drag questions to reorder them, or use the up/down controls. Duplicate, delete, preview, change media, and save questions to your reusable library. Publishing updates future deliveries; an existing session retains the version it started with.

Games can be renamed, duplicated, tagged, organized in course folders, archived, restored or deleted. Templates are reusable copies. Export JSON to preserve complete game settings and media references. CSV and Excel import templates are available in My Games. Invalid or unsupported content is reported before saving.

Scores and written-answer review

Standard questions award up to 1,000 points, double-point questions up to 2,000. Speed reduces the accuracy component from full points toward half points; streak bonuses are capped within the question's maximum. Partial credit subtracts wrong selections from correct selections before dividing by the number of correct answers. Polls and discussion formats award zero.

Unmatched reviewed text waits for you to choose full, half or zero credit. Grade it during reveal or from the report. Reports use the latest attempt for class accuracy and scores; named exports also retain earlier submissions. Unanswered graded questions count as incorrect. A shared tie gives equal ranks; other tie settings use response time or joining order.

Presentation and student access

Host settings control sound, music, motion, student question visibility, randomization, automatic progression, late arrivals, explanations, rankings and student name requirements. Choose a name prompt and length range, or require a two-part name for named sessions. Automatic progression waits eight seconds after reveal. Use full screen for projection; Mute silences audio. Keyboard controls, visible focus, answer letters/shapes, image descriptions and reduced-motion support are included.

A returning student should use the same browser and joining link. The secure browser cookie restores their identity and score. Clearing cookies or changing devices creates a new identity; attempt limits apply per browser. Anonymous games assign participant numbers and omit individual records from reports. Ask students to avoid sensitive information in written responses.

Media, retention and troubleshooting

Upload PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, MP3, WAV, OGG, MP4 or WebM files up to 50 MB. Videos can also use YouTube links. Uploaded classroom media uses public URLs; portable exports reference these files rather than embedding copies.

Settings → Report retention controls how long expired or completed sessions are kept (90 days by default). Cleanup runs when you open the dashboard. You can delete an individual report at any time. Download results before deleting them.

If a connection drops, use Reconnect or reload the same joining link. Retry a failed answer submission without changing it. Draft conflicts protect edits made in another tab: export the retained draft, reopen the game and restore the intended changes. Resume an interrupted instructor session from Host Live Game.

The verified classroom target is 100 simultaneous participants. A 150-participant safety cap is configured; capacity beyond the tested target is not claimed.
