HOW IT WORKS

One leader. Multiple perspectives.

1 · CreateAn administrator starts a private assessment cycle.
2 · InviteSelf, supervisor, peers, and direct reports receive unique links.
3 · ReflectEvaluators rate observable leadership behaviors from their perspective.
4 · DevelopResults reveal patterns, strengths, gaps, and development opportunities.
Confidentiality matters. Individual feedback is designed to be reported through role-based aggregation and confidentiality safeguards. The 360° is a developmental feedback experience, not a diagnostic or employment-selection instrument.
