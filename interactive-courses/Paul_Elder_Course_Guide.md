# Critical Thinking: Reason Better, Decide Better

An original Mayne Leadership course based on Richard Paul and Linda Elder’s critical thinking framework.

Designed for adult learners, instructors, and workplace leaders. Allow approximately two hours, including workbook activities and a capstone decision brief. The same course supports individual study or facilitated delivery.

## Open and edit

- On the website, open Interactive Course Studio → Course library → Critical Thinking → Open editable copy.
- Alternatively, use Import project to open `Critical_Thinking_Paul_Elder.mayne-course.json` from the download package.
- Edit text directly on the canvas. Slides, feedback layers, button states, response variables, and triggers are native Studio objects.
- Preview course uses the Studio’s shared interaction engine. Instructor prompts are in slide notes.
- Download project preserves an editable backup. Use Save to website when the Studio’s project storage is available. The built-in course library itself does not depend on that storage.
- Native Export HTML preserves the slides and their interactions. The supplied `Critical_Thinking_Paul_Elder.html` adds a course map, reading view, and workbook around the same engine. Those companion features are part of the supplied learner package rather than the native export interface.

## Run the learner course

Open `Critical_Thinking_Paul_Elder.html` in a browser. It is self-contained and does not need an application installation or a network connection to run. Official reference links need internet access.

Use the course map or Previous/Next controls. Reading view presents the same slide content and response buttons in a layout that adapts to smaller screens. It is the initial view on phones. My workbook stores writing in the browser when storage is available; Save workbook as text and Print workbook provide portable copies.

Slide progress and assessment responses are retained on the device when browser storage is available. Restart resets the course attempt. Workbook writing remains until Clear my writing is selected. Device data is not shared with an instructor or sent to an LMS.

## Facilitation

Begin by asking for a decision someone revised after seeing new evidence. Explain that the course will examine participants’ own reasoning as well as other people’s claims.

The Client Services Desk case starts with a fall in the timely rate and an unsupported causal explanation. The request-mix update lets learners revise their analysis. Do not declare that case mix explains every delay: complex-case performance also fell, and process evidence is still needed.

The ClearPath capstone is a separate fictional case. Its favorable pilot result is limited by differences in case mix, volunteer selection, short observation, and missing quality measures. The duplicate-closure update introduces an accuracy problem. Ask learners to reconcile the numerator and denominator before presenting a corrected rate.

Use paired discussion to examine a competing explanation or viewpoint. Invite a justified recommendation with explicit uncertainty. A bounded pilot is one possible action; a pause or expansion can also be defended if the learner addresses evidence, consequences, and safeguards convincingly.

## Capstone review rubric

Score each criterion 0–2: 0 = absent; 1 = partial or asserted; 2 = specific and supported.

| Criterion | Look for |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and question | A stated outcome and bounded decision question |
| Evidence and accuracy | Correct use of figures and discussion of duplicates and comparability |
| Information versus inference | A clear separation of records from causal or predictive conclusions |
| Assumptions and tests | Two important assumptions and useful checks |
| Intellectual standards | Three named standards applied to specific reasoning |
| Viewpoints and fairness | A fair competing perspective and consistent evidence rules |
| Consequences and action | Benefits, risks, stakeholder effects, and a proportionate action |
| Revision and intellectual traits | Limits acknowledged and a meaningful trigger for reassessment |

A suggested instructor review benchmark is 12/16. Workbook ratings are self-review; they do not automatically grade the brief.

## Learning assessment and completion

The assessment has 12 applied questions, choice-specific feedback, and unlimited opportunities to reconsider. Each question stores its latest response. Repeat clicks do not add points; Results recomputes the total.

Complete course is enabled only when all 12 questions have responses, at least 10 answers are correct, and the learner has confirmed completing and reviewing the capstone. This confirmation records the learner’s own statement; it is not an instructor judgment of the brief. Assessment and completion are device-based learning records, not secure LMS grades or a formal Paul–Elder certification. SCORM and Learning Studio grade reporting are outside the Studio foundation’s current scope.
