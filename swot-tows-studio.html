TRANSPARENT WEIGHTED DECISION SUPPORT

Prioritize the strategies worth acting on.

Score each recommendation on a 1–5 scale. Set the importance of four criteria, then compare the calculated ranks. For resource demand, 1 means easier and 5 means more demanding.

Scoring weights

100 total

Score = weighted average of Impact, Feasibility, Urgency, and (6 − Resources). Results are decision aids—not forecasts.
