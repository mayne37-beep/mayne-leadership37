Who is your leadership team?

Your role in the simulation
You are not playing assigned characters. You are collectively advising the leader of the country you create. Your own judgment drives what your government does.
Step 2

Create your country

Make it distinctive enough that your team feels ownership of it. These details may become relevant later.
Step 3

What does your nation stand for?

Choose characteristics that matter enough that your leadership team would be reluctant to violate them—even during a serious crisis.
Do not optimize for the game.
You do not know what will happen next. Build the country your team wants to represent.
Step 4

Determine your starting capability

Countries do not begin equally. Roll two six-sided dice. Your national capability pool is 10 + the dice total.
